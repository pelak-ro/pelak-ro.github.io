Privacy Policy

Reskin Studio · last updated 2 November 2026

What the app is

Reskin Studio is an internal tool used by our game team. It runs entirely on a team member's own computer. There is no server operated by the developer, and the app has no user accounts of its own.

What it does with your Google account

When you choose to connect Google Drive, you sign in with Google and grant access to Google Drive. The app uses that access only to:

  • upload the team's approved and finished work (pictures, 3D models, and the tool's settings and catalogues) to the team's shared Google Drive folder;
  • download the team's files and tool updates from that folder.

It does not read, change or delete any other files in your Drive, and it never deletes files from Drive.

What is stored, and where

The sign-in token Google issues is saved only on your own computer, in your user profile's settings folder. It is not sent to the developer or anyone else. The app collects no personal information, no usage data and no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Sign out on the app's Home tab to delete the token from your computer. To revoke the app's access entirely, remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
