Reskin Studio

An internal tool of our game team.

Reskin Studio runs on a team member's own computer. It helps the team make art for a Roblox game: it generates pictures, lets a person approve them, and turns approved pictures into textured 3D models.

When a team member connects their Google account, Reskin Studio backs up approved and finished work to the team's shared Google Drive folder, downloads the team's files from it, and keeps the tool itself up to date from it. It only works with that folder.
